Kaimur Bihar Community Computer Education, known as KBC Computer Education, offers a range of computer-focused learning options in Kaimur, Bihar.

Teaching is available in Hindi and English. The course range includes CCC, DCA, DFA, ADCA, Typing Master, Prime Tally, Tally ERP 0.9, C+ and DCTT.
